# Writing rules

- No em or en dashes in prose. Use a comma, a period, or parentheses.
- Every sentence earns its place. Lead with the point. Prefer short sentences and bullets.
- Link every PR, issue, file, build or doc on first mention, pinned to a commit when it documents a specific state. Never guess a path, line or ID.
- Inclusive terms: primary or main, replica or follower, allowlist, denylist.
